# DESIGN.md - Chisel Industries

## Thematic Story: "The Chisel Blueprint"
> The Chisel Industries design language is a cinematic fusion of industrial precision and raw craftsmanship. It moves away from the sterile "tech" look, embracing the textures of the built world—wood, steel, and stone—while maintaining a high-contrast, premium digital interface. It is the visual equivalent of a master craftsman’s blueprint: sharp, intentional, and enduring.

**Theme:** dark industrial
